## Description
DeskTodo is a calm desktop widget that keeps your day in one place, without opening a website.

AT A GLANCE
• A big, clear clock with today's date, sunrise and sunset
• Your next meeting and how soon it starts
• Weather for two places, with feels-like, humidity, wind, rain chance, UV and the next six hours
• A month calendar you can swipe through; pick any date to see its meetings and tasks

TO-DO
• Today at the top, then the rest of the week
• Sub-tasks under any task
• Unfinished tasks move to the next day automatically at midnight

NOTES
• Quick notes with bold text, headings and bullet lists
• Everything saves as you type

AND MORE
• Light and dark themes
• Borderless widget look with rounded corners, or a normal window
• Keep it on top of other windows
• Connect your Outlook calendar with its published ICS link (optional)
• No account. Your tasks and notes stay on your PC.

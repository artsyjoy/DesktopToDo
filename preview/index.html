Preview build

DeskTodo

This is the real DeskTodo interface running in the same engine as the desktop app, with sample tasks, notes, weather and meetings. Try anything; nothing here touches your own data.

New in this build

  • Analog clock redrawn after the Braun: the numerals sit inside the ring of minute marks, in place of the hour marks
  • Longer minute hand reaching into the marks, a shorter hour hand, and a soft luminous stripe near each tip
  • Switch to Analog under the clock to see it

Not in the preview

Live weather, your real calendars, backups and window controls (pin, close, resize) only work in the desktop app.

# DeskTodo privacy policy

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

DeskTodo is a desktop to-do list, notes and "at a glance" widget. It is built to keep your information on
your own computer.

## What DeskTodo stores

Your tasks, notes, settings, chosen weather locations, and (if you connect one) your calendar link are
saved in a file on your PC. DeskTodo has no accounts and no servers of its own, and does not send this
information to the developer or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

## Internet access

DeskTodo only goes online for two things:

- **Weather.** To show the weather it sends the latitude and longitude of the places you picked to
  [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (and the place name you type when you search for a city).
  Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
- **Calendar (optional).** If you paste a published Outlook calendar (ICS) link, DeskTodo downloads that
  calendar from Microsoft to show your upcoming events. The link and the events are kept only on your PC.

DeskTodo does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or cookies, and does not collect personal data.

## Children

DeskTodo does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## DeskTodo 1.5.6

### New: lives on your desktop
DeskTodo now behaves like a desktop widget by default: it stays **behind your apps**, and it's there
whenever you go to the desktop (including **Win+D** / Show desktop). Click it to use it; switch back to an app
and it slips behind again.

Choose where it lives: right-click the top bar →
- **Stay on desktop (behind apps)** — the new default
- **Stay on top of apps** — the pin button (or Ctrl+T) switches between this and desktop
- **Normal window**

If you had "Keep on top" turned on before, it stays on top until you change it.

### Install
1. Close DeskTodo (if needed: **Task Manager** → **DeskTodo** → **End task**).
2. Download **DeskTodo-v1.5.6-windows.zip** below, extract it, and replace your **DeskTodo** folder.
3. Run **DeskTodo.exe**. Your tasks and notes are kept. The right-click menu should say **DeskTodo 1.5.6**.

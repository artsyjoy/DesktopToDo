## DeskTodo 1.5.8

### New: all your calendars in one view
- Connect **as many calendars as you like**, from Google Calendar, Outlook or both. Their events are merged
  into the month calendar, "Up next" and "This week". A meeting that appears in two calendars shows once.
- Open **This week → ··· → Calendars…** to add, rename or remove calendars. Each calendar's own name
  (e.g. "Work_Meetings") is filled in automatically.
- If one calendar can't be loaded, the others still show, and the list marks the one that isn't loading.
- Your existing calendar link is kept and appears as the first calendar in the list.

**Google Calendar:** Google gives each calendar its own link. In Google Calendar's settings, click a
calendar under "Settings for my calendars", scroll to "Integrate calendar", and copy **Secret address in
iCal format**. Repeat this for each calendar. For "Holidays in United States", use the
**Public address in iCal format**.

### Install
1. Close DeskTodo (if needed: **Task Manager** → **DeskTodo** → **End task**).
2. Download **DeskTodo-v1.5.8-windows.zip** below, extract it, and replace your **DeskTodo** folder.
3. Run **DeskTodo.exe**. Your tasks and notes are kept. The right-click menu should say **DeskTodo 1.5.8**.

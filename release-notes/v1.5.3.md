## DeskTodo 1.5.3

### New
- **Drag to rearrange tasks.** In To-do (and in the calendar's day panel), drag a task up or down to reorder it,
  drag it right to make it a sub-task of the one above, left to make it a main task again,
  or drop it on another day's name to move it to that day.
- **Drag to rearrange notes.** Hover a line and a handle appears on its left. Drag it to move the line
  (a bullet brings everything nested under it). Drag right to turn a line into a bullet or nest it, left to un-nest.

### Polish
- Long meeting links and locations in **This week** stay on one line inside the card, ending in "…" with a soft fade.

### Install
1. Close DeskTodo (if needed: **Task Manager** → **DeskTodo** → **End task**).
2. Download **DeskTodo-v1.5.3-windows.zip** below, extract it, and replace your **DeskTodo** folder.
3. Run **DeskTodo.exe**. Your tasks and notes are kept.

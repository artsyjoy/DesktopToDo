## DeskTodo 1.5.9

### New
- **Analog clock.** Switch between **Digital** and **Analog** under the clock (also in Settings). The analog
  face has minute marks and numerals, plain hour and minute hands, and a second hand in your accent color
  that sweeps smoothly instead of ticking.
- **Accent color.** The new gear button in the top bar opens **Settings**. Pick from 10 colors, or any color
  with the rainbow swatch. "Reset color" goes back to orange. Text on light colors such as yellow switches to
  dark so it stays readable.

### Changed
- The digital clock shows hours above minutes, with no seconds. In 12-hour mode the hour keeps its leading
  zero (08) and AM/PM sits beside it.
- The 12h/24h switch stays in the same place in both formats.
- The calendar on "At a glance" shows only your events. Tasks stay in the To-do tab.

### Install
1. Close DeskTodo (if needed: **Task Manager** → **DeskTodo** → **End task**).
2. Download **DeskTodo-v1.5.9-windows.zip** below, extract it, and replace your **DeskTodo** folder.
3. Run **DeskTodo.exe**. Your tasks, notes and calendars are kept. The right-click menu should say **DeskTodo 1.5.9**.

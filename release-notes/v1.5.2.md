## DeskTodo 1.5.2

Fixes "DeskTodo couldn't open its window" (FileLoadException, 0x80131515) when the zip wasn't unblocked
before unzipping. DeskTodo now clears Windows' "downloaded from the internet" mark from its own files
when it starts, so it opens either way.

Also includes 1.5.1: one DeskTodo at a time, clearer startup errors, and a startup log at
`%APPDATA%\DeskTodo\desktodo.log`.

### Install
1. Close DeskTodo (if needed: **Task Manager** → **DeskTodo** → **End task**).
2. Download **DeskTodo-v1.5.2-windows.zip** below, unzip it, and replace your **DeskTodo** folder.
3. Run **DeskTodo.exe**. Your tasks and notes are kept.

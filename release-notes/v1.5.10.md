## DeskTodo 1.5.10

### New analog clock face
A minimalist watch face:
- a full ring of fine minute marks, with longer marks for the hours and no numerals
- tapered, faceted hour and minute hands that cast a soft shadow on the dial, meeting at a raised center hub
- a hairline second hand in your accent color that sweeps smoothly

### New accent colors and color picker
- Swatches: orange (the new default), yellow, red, blue, navy, green, sand and light grey.
- The rainbow swatch opens a built-in color picker: a rounded shade square, a hue slider and a hex field.
  The widget changes color live as you drag.
- With very pale colors on the light theme, filled shapes get a thin outline so they stay visible. With very
  dark colors on the dark theme, accent text is lightened so it stays readable.

### Install
1. Close DeskTodo (if needed: **Task Manager** → **DeskTodo** → **End task**).
2. Download **DeskTodo-v1.5.10-windows.zip** below, extract it, and replace your **DeskTodo** folder.
3. Run **DeskTodo.exe**. Your tasks, notes and calendars are kept. The right-click menu should say **DeskTodo 1.5.10**.

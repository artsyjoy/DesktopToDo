## DeskTodo 1.5.11

### Fixed: analog clock showing the wrong time
The analog hands were right when DeskTodo opened, but drifted ahead after the first minute, and were far off
after switching tabs. Once a minute the app re-aligned the hands, and that step accidentally added the
time the widget had been open. The hands are now checked against the real time every second and set
directly, so they always match the digital clock.

### Install
1. Close DeskTodo (if needed: **Task Manager** → **DeskTodo** → **End task**).
2. Download **DeskTodo-v1.5.11-windows.zip** below, extract it, and replace your **DeskTodo** folder.
3. Run **DeskTodo.exe**. Your tasks, notes and calendars are kept. The right-click menu should say **DeskTodo 1.5.11**.

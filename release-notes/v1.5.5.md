## DeskTodo 1.5.5

Fixes the window freezing (and needing **End task**) while resizing it from the handle on the bottom edge.
Resizing now follows the mouse natively, smoothly, and stops at the bottom of your screen.

### Install
1. Close DeskTodo (if needed: **Task Manager** → **DeskTodo** → **End task**).
2. Download **DeskTodo-v1.5.5-windows.zip** below, extract it, and replace your **DeskTodo** folder.
3. Run **DeskTodo.exe** from the new folder. Your tasks and notes are kept.
   Right-click the top bar to confirm it says **DeskTodo 1.5.5** at the bottom of the menu.
